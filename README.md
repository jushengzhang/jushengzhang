I am a **Visiting Researcher at Stanford University** and a **Student Researcher at Google DeepMind**. My research connects reliable agents, efficient visual intelligence, and embodied decision-making.

My goal is to help build the next generation of **AI agents for scientific discovery**.

### Research interests

- **Agents** — multi-agent collaboration, uncertainty-aware reasoning, and learning from failures.
- **Vision-language models** — token compression, robust visual reasoning, and efficient video representations.
- **Robotics** — cooperative planning, world-action modeling, and embodied intelligence.
- **AI for science** — agents that support scientific research and discovery.
